# Playbook — Nutra (weight-loss / skin / health, incl. COD)

Status (reviewed 2026-08-28): directional vendor benchmarks (dated below) + verified Meta policy.
NOT this team's live data — replace numbers with your own once you have them.

Funnel: FB/IG ad → pre-lander (advertorial / quiz) → offer page. Lead = CPA
network conversion; COD = order confirmed by call center (the real payout gate).

## Event ladder & KPI

click → LP view → lead/order (form submit) → **COD confirm** (call-center
approved) → shipped/paid. Primary KPI = **confirmed CPA = ad spend ÷ confirmed
orders** (NOT submitted-lead CPL). Don't confuse with payout ÷ confirmed orders
= revenue per confirmed order; margin/order = payout − confirmed CPA. Quality
delay: confirm happens hours–days after the order, so today's lead volume is
provisional; judge on the confirmed cohort, not same-day submits.

Your MEDIA CPA (what you PAY Meta per confirmed order) is a different number and
must stay below payout for margin — don't read the payout row as your cost.

T3 confirm consistently below T1/T2 (qualitative; no clean aggregate). Confirm
rate is the hidden multiplier of real CPA — a $17 payout at 25% confirm needs
lead CPL ≤ ~$4.25 to break even.

## GEO/device

COD GEOs (LATAM, EU-t2, SEA) behave differently from straight-sale T1 — the call
center + native-operator language quality moves confirm more than creative.
Mobile-first LPs; heavy pre-lander.

## Creative concepts & first tests

Angles: advertorial ("doctor/local-news" story), quiz ("answer 3 questions"),
before/after avoided in-ad (policy, below) → transformation lives on the LP.
First test: 2-3 pre-lander angles × 1 broad adset each (1-3-1 = directional
screening, not a causal test — see 04); optimise LP→lead
before scaling. Spy: AdSpy / Anstrex (Anstrex rips native nutra pre-landers).

## Common failure modes

- Optimising to submitted leads while confirm silently drops → CPA looks great,
  P&L bleeds. Always reconcile to confirmed.
- Cheap-CPL GEO with 15% confirm loses to pricier GEO at 45%.
- Pre-lander claim too strong → account/domain death (policy).

## Moderation (docs-level, verified Aug 2026)

- Unsafe supplements = outright prohibited (anabolic steroids, chitosan,
  comfrey, DHEA, ephedra, HGH...). Rx drugs need licensing evidence.
- Before/after under Health & Wellness: non-product weight-loss side-by-sides
  and idealized transformations prohibited; no negative-self-perception content.
  Exact cosmetic before/after wording = PARTIALLY VERIFIED (confirm live). Hence
  neutral images + claims on the pre-lander.
- Personal Attributes: copy can't imply the reader's health/condition
  ("Struggling with your weight?") — reframe to product benefit.

## Metrics discipline

- Lead = the CPA network's CONFIRMED event per the team's definition — pin down
  EXACTLY which tracker status/measure that is before any CPL math (tracker-ops
  metric rule). Confirm arrives on a lag → cohort by click date (tracker-ops 01).
