# 10 — Restricted verticals: permission, conditions, or no path

Fetched live from `transparency.meta.com/policies/ad-standards/...` 2026-08-27 (raw HTML is a JS shell —
must render). **[official]** unless labeled. 🔺 = revised multiple times a year, re-check before spend.

**Read Q1 and Q2 first. They are the only two questions that decide whether to build anything.**

## Q1 — Permission required BEFORE spend

Launching without it is a **policy violation**, not a rejected creative. It counts against the account.

| Vertical | What must exist first |
|---|---|
| Real-money gambling (casino, sportsbook, poker, third-party lottery, DFS, sweepstakes casino, skill tournament) | A&V approval + per-jurisdiction licence proof + **declared intent per new territory** |
| Crypto exchanges/trading, lending/borrowing, wallets that buy·sell·swap·stake, mining **software**, investment solicitation (incl. affiliate sites) | Written permission + recognized regulator licence **per targeted country** |
| Dating services (any monetized matchmaking or aggregator) | Prior written permission — separate application form, `facebook.com/help/contact/1060028634014002` |
| Prescription drugs, online pharmacy, telehealth prescribing named Rx, pharma manufacturers | Active **LegitScript** certification (or Meta internal review) + Meta authorization |
| CBD | **LegitScript CBD** certification + Meta written authorization |
| Political / social-issue / electoral in any designated country | Identity authorization + verified "Paid for by" disclaimer, live before the ad runs |
| Financial products where local law demands a licence (insurance, mortgages, loans, investment products, credit cards) | Regulator licence proof, subject to Meta review |

Everything else is either **self-compliance** (conditions apply, no submission: alcohol, weight loss,
supplements, weapon-adjacent gear, subscriptions, MLM) or **prohibited** (no permission exists).

## Q2 — No path, any geo, any permission. Stop; do not iterate creative

- **Tobacco / nicotine / vapes**, incl. nicotine-free "wellness" vapes, pouches, paraphernalia, and
  tobacco-brand promotion. Policy words it "not allowed in any context."
- **Firearms, ammunition, explosives**, weapon-modification accessories (suppressors, optics), and
  **3D-print/CAM firearm files "regardless of context or poster."**
- **Payday loans, paycheck advances, bail bonds, any loan term ≤90 days.**
- **Binary options, CFDs, penny/bidding-fee auctions, ICOs.**
- **THC / psychoactive cannabis**, ingestible or not.
- **Adult sexual arousal products/services** — toys, erotic content/costumes, non-surgical genital
  enhancement, strip clubs, escort/prostitution-adjacent, tantric/"orgasmic therapy."
- **Dating involving a monetary transaction** (mail-order-bride models) — unpermittable even holding a
  dating grant.
- **Political/social-issue/electoral into the EU** — structural geo-block since the Jul 2025 policy
  change; only election-authority voting-logistics ads survive.
- **Gambling of any kind into the 19 unsupported markets**, at any authorization level (below).

## Gambling — the details that decide the build

**Definition is deliberately wide:** "any product or service where anything of monetary value is included
as part of a method of entry and prize." Catches betting, lotteries, raffles, casino, fantasy sports incl.
DFS, bingo, poker, skill tournaments, sweepstakes, and **trial-then-pay games**.

**19 unsupported markets** — no gambling or social-casino ads at all, authorization irrelevant:
Azerbaijan · Bangladesh · Cambodia · Egypt · Hong Kong · India · Indonesia · Korea · Kyrgyzstan ·
Malaysia · Mongolia · Myanmar · Pakistan · Philippines · Saudi Arabia · Singapore · Taiwan · Thailand ·
Vietnam. 🔺 List confirmed live 2026-08-27; the "Feb 2026 expansion" date some practitioners cite could
not be tied to a dated official announcement.

**Allowed without permission:** brick-and-mortar venue ads whose destination doesn't facilitate online
play · state/government lotteries where the advertiser **is** the direct operator · retail prize
promotions where gambling isn't the core business · genuinely free-to-play non-simulating games.

**Social casino carve-out:** no gambling authorization needed if (a) no payout of monetary value ever,
(b) 18+ targeting, (c) not the 19 markets.

**Affiliates and aggregators are not exempt** — the landing page counts as a gambling ad even with no
on-page mechanic. 18+ floor everywhere. Article: `.../restricted-goods-services/gambling-games/`,
Help Center 345214789920228.

## Crypto — the exemption boundary

Permission: exchanges/trading (spot, margin, futures) · lending/borrowing · wallets with buy·sell·swap·
stake · mining **software** · investment solicitation and aggregator/affiliate sites.
No permission: tax services · education/news/events · **NFTs and non-currency blockchain products** ·
**storage-only wallets** · mining **hardware**.

~25 jurisdictions have a named regulator/licence path (US: FinCEN MSB or NY BitLicense · UK: FCA ·
Singapore: MAS DPT under the Payment Services Act…). 🔺 Whether unlisted countries are silently
unsupported or merely undocumented is **not stated** — treat as unsupported until Meta publishes a path.
Changelog: Apr 2026 / Nov 2025 / Jul 2024 / Mar 2023 — revised repeatedly.
Article: `.../restricted-goods-services/cryptocurrency-products-and-services/`, Help Center 438252513416690.

## Health, weight loss, pharma

18+ mandatory on weight loss / weight gain / cosmetic. **Banned outright:** statements of physical-
appearance inferiority · close-up fat-pinching imagery · "results from a wearable alone" · permanent
skin-colour-change claims · cure/heal claims for diabetes, herpes, thyroid, psoriasis, Ebola, cancer,
autism, Alzheimer's, Parkinson's, ALS, HIV (**symptom-management claims are fine** — that's the line) ·
clickbait/exaggerated timeframes without qualifiers.

Before/after imagery is **explicitly allowed for general cosmetic procedures**. 🔺 For **weight loss**
specifically the policy says neither yes nor no — the negative-self-perception ban is what bites. Treat
as high-risk, not as a carve-out.

Supplements: the operative text is "unsafe substances, products or supplements, **as determined by Meta
at its sole discretion**." No published rubric. There is no bright line to engineer against.

## Financial — what's actually prohibited vs merely conditional

Prohibited by name: payday/paycheck-advance/bail-bond/≤90-day loans · binary options · CFDs ·
penny/bidding-fee auctions · misleading student-loan consolidation/forgiveness claims.
Conditional: credit cards, insurance, loans >90 days — 18+, statutory disclosures, and **no PII or
financial-info capture inside the ad itself** (bank/card/routing numbers banned under Privacy Violations).
US extra: investment-product ads may **not** invite DM interaction on or off platform.
No authorization needed: bank/insurer brand ads · news that doesn't offer the product · educational
content · a mere mention with no way to obtain.

Credit repair / debt relief: no dedicated article. Banned claims — erase or reduce debt by a set
amount/timeframe · stop all collections or lawsuits · a "new government program" · delete credit-report
entries · a "new credit identity."

🔺 **Forex and non-binary options trading:** practitioner blogs claim a licence-authorization path. The
official Financial Services and Prohibited Financial Products pages, as fetched, name only binary options
and CFDs and are **silent on forex**. Restricted-pending-verification — neither confirmed prohibited nor
confirmed permission-gated. Do not advise either way without a live check.

## Subscriptions — a real policy, recently written

Ads collecting PII are **prohibited** if any of: no opt-in checkbox, a **pre-checked** one, no clear
cancellation instructions, or price/billing interval not clearly shown (buried in fine print, a privacy
statement, or behind a separate link). No submission — pure self-compliance plus review-time detection.
🔺 The page's changelog showed only "Today" at fetch, so it is new or just revised.

## Political — beyond the authorization

EU: **not allowed at all** since Jul 2025, except election-authority voting-logistics ads.
US, Brazil, India, Israel, Mexico, UK: additional ban on vote discouragement, questioning election
legitimacy, and premature victory claims.
Washington State: **total ban** on WA state/local election content and Seattle legislation, authorization
or not.
AI-generated realistic media must be self-disclosed; **from 2026-06-01 Meta auto-detects and labels**
undisclosed AI media. Ads stored in Ad Library **7 years**.

## Gaps — do not guess

1. Full country list of the alcohol ad ban (fetch truncated after Afghanistan, Brunei, Bangladesh, Egypt).
2. Whether forex / non-binary options need a distinct authorization path — official pages silent.
3. Submission mechanism for credit-repair and debt-settlement licence verification — requirement and
   banned claims are documented, the intake flow is not.
4. Meta's internal rubric for "sole discretion" on unsafe supplements — none published.
5. Weight-loss before/after imagery — allowed or banned, neither stated.
6. Whether SIEP carries an advertiser **residency** requirement beyond "located in or targeting."
7. Effective date of the current 19-market gambling list.
8. Whether crypto-unlisted countries are unsupported or merely undocumented.
